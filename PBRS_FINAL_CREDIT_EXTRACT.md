## SECTION 1: PW - PRECIOUS WATER
**Total Points Available:** 43 | **Credits:** 8 (2 Required + 6 Optional)

### PW-1 - Improved Interior Water Use Reduction (15 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level  
- **0 pts:** Not awarded
- **1 pt:** 4% potable water reduction vs. baseline
- **2 pts:** 8% reduction
- **3 pts:** 12% reduction
- **4 pts:** 16% reduction
- **5 pts:** 20% reduction
- **6 pts:** 24% reduction
- **7 pts:** 28% reduction
- **8 pts:** 32% reduction
- **9 pts:** 36% reduction
- **10 pts:** 40% reduction
- **11 pts:** 44% reduction
- **12 pts:** 48% reduction
- **13 pts:** 52% reduction
- **14 pts:** 56% reduction
- **15 pts:** 60% potable water reduction vs. baseline

**Award Criteria:** Achieve further reductions in proposed building potable water consumption compared to baseline using Building Water Calculator

---

### PW-2.1 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Landscaping (8-10 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level (four independent award paths with max 8-10 pts total)
- **Plant Selection (0-4 pts):**
  - 2 pts: Average landscape irrigation demand < 4 liters/m²/day
  - 4 pts: Average landscape irrigation demand < 2 liters/m²/day
- **Irrigation System (1 pt):**
  - 1 pt: Water Efficient Irrigation System incorporated
- **System Management (1 pt):**
  - 1 pt: Irrigation Operation & Maintenance Plan developed
- **Recycled Water (2 pts):**
  - 2 pts: 100% of irrigation demand served via Exterior Water Allowance + recycled water mainline loop (if not available immediately)
- **Schools Only (2 pts):**
  - 2 pts: Non-water turf substitutes installed for playing fields

**Cumulative Maximum:** 8-10 pts

---

### PW-2.2 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Heat Rejection (8 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level (two distinct award pathways)
**Water-Based Heat Rejection (2-5 pts):**
- **2 pts:** > 25% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up served via Exterior Water Allowance
- **3 pts:** > 50% served
- **4 pts:** > 75% served
- **5 pts:** 100% served

**Alternative - No Water for Heat Rejection (8 pts):**
- **8 pts:** Project uses no water for heat rejection (passive/alternative systems)

**Award Criteria:** Demonstrate reduced potable water for heat rejection through recycled water or eliminate water-based cooling entirely

---

### PW-2.3 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Water Features (4 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level (three distinct award pathways)
**Option 1 - Recycled Water for Features (2 pts):**
- **2 pts:** Water make-up for all exterior water features served via Exterior Water Allowance

**Option 2 - Pool Blankets (1 pt):**
- **1 pt:** All external swimming pools supplied with permanently installed retractable pool blankets

**Option 3 - No Water Features (4 pts):**
- **4 pts:** No exterior water features or swimming pools on site

**Award Criteria:** Reduce potable water for water features through recycled water, pool covers, or elimination

---

### PW-R1 - Minimum Interior Water Use Reduction (0 pts - REQUIRED)
**Structure:** Binary  
- **0 pts:** Demonstrate building's predicted interior potable water consumption ≤ baseline using efficiency measures only

---

### PW-R2 - Exterior Water Monitoring (0 pts - REQUIRED)
**Structure:** Binary  
- **0 pts:** Provide easily accessible, clearly labeled water meters for: heat rejection, external hose bibs, irrigation systems, swimming pools, water features with central monitoring system capable of hourly/daily/weekly/monthly/annual tracking

---

### SM-1 - Non-Polluting Materials (3 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level (multiple pathways, 0-3 pts cumulative)
**Path 1 - Zero ODP, Low GWP Insulation (1 pt):**
- **1 pt:** All thermal insulation has ODP = 0 AND Global Warming Potential < 5

**Path 2 - Chlorine Free Materials (1 pt):**
- **1 pt:** Replace chlorine-based materials (PVC, CPE, CPVC, CSPE, polychloroprene) with sustainable alternatives for 3+ product groups at specified coverage:
  - Insulation: 80% or more by surface area
  - Waterproofing: 50% or more
  - External cladding: 90% or more
  - Floor/wall coverings: 90% or more

**Path 3 - Mercury Free (1 pt):**
- **1 pt:** Eliminate mercury from all building materials (lighting, controls, etc.)

**Cumulative Maximum:** 3 pts

---

### SM-2 - Design for Materials Reduction (1 pt)
