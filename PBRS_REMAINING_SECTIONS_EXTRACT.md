## SECTION 1: PW - PRECIOUS WATER
**Section Maximum Points:** 43 | **Total Credits:** 8 (2 Required + 6 Optional)

### PW-R1 - Minimum Interior Water Use Reduction (0 pts)
**Type:** Required | **Structure:** Binary (0/0)
- **0 pts:** Required credit - no points awarded
- **Award Level Criteria:** Demonstrate building's predicted interior potable water consumption ≤ baseline building consumption using efficiency measures only

### PW-R2 - Exterior Water Monitoring (0 pts)
**Type:** Required | **Structure:** Binary (0/0)
- **0 pts:** Required credit - no points awarded
- **Award Level Criteria:** Provide water meters capable of monitoring heat rejection, external hose bibs, irrigation systems, swimming pools, and water features with data logging and central monitoring

### PW-2.1 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Landscaping (8-10 pts)
**Type:** Optional | **Structure:** Multi-level (multiple criteria, 0-10 pts total)
**Maximum Design Rating:** 8-10 pts | **Maximum Construction Rating:** 8-10 pts

**Point Breakdown:**
- **0 pts:** Not awarded
- **2 pts:** Average landscape irrigation demand < 4 liters/m²/day
- **4 pts:** Average landscape irrigation demand < 2 liters/m²/day
- **1 pt:** Water Efficient Irrigation System incorporated
- **1 pt:** Irrigation Operation & Maintenance Plan developed
- **2 pts:** 100% of exterior irrigation demand served using Exterior Water Allowance + recycled water loop
- **2 pts (Schools only):** Non-water turf substitutes installed in place of turf

**Cumulative Maximum:** 8-10 pts depending on combination

---

### PW-2.2 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Heat Rejection (8 pts)
**Type:** Optional | **Structure:** Multi-level (0/2-5 or 8 pts)
**Maximum Design Rating:** 8 pts | **Maximum Construction Rating:** 8 pts

**Point Structure - Water-Based Heat Rejection:**
- **0 pts:** Not awarded
- **2 pts:** > 25% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up requirements served using Exterior Water Allowance
- **3 pts:** > 50% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up requirements served
- **4 pts:** > 75% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up requirements served
- **5 pts:** 100% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up requirements served

**Alternative:**
- **8 pts:** No water used for heat rejection (passive cooling or alternative methods)

---

### PW-2.3 - Exterior Water Use Reduction: Water Features (4 pts)
**Type:** Optional | **Structure:** Multi-level (0/1-2 or 4 pts)
**Maximum Design Rating:** 4 pts | **Maximum Construction Rating:** 4 pts

**Point Structure:**
- **0 pts:** Not awarded
- **2 pts:** Water make-up requirements for all exterior water features served using Exterior Water Allowance
- **1 pt:** All external swimming pools supplied with permanently installed retractable pool blankets

**Alternative:**
- **4 pts:** No exterior water features or swimming pools on site
