## SECTION 1: IDP - INTEGRATED DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
**Total Section Points:** 13 | **Credits:** 9 (6 Required + 3 Optional)

### IDP-R1: Integrated Development Strategy (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Establish an integrated team approach to sustainability throughout the development process
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Establish integrated development team with defined roles and sustainability objectives

### IDP-R2: Tenant Fit-Out Design & Construction Guide (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Guide tenant design and construction practices to minimize environmental impact
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Provide tenant fit-out guidelines addressing sustainability practices

### IDP-R3: Basic Commissioning (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Verify that building systems perform as designed
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Implement basic commissioning plan for building systems

**Structure:** Binary

**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded

- 0 pts: Not awarded

**Structure:** Binary

**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded

## SECTION 5: PW - PRECIOUS WATER
**Total Section Points:** 43 | **Credits:** 8 (2 Required + 6 Optional)

### PW-R1: Minimum Interior Water Use Reduction (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Reduce building potable water consumption through efficiency measures
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Demonstrate building's predicted interior potable water consumption ≤ baseline building consumption using efficiency measures only

### PW-R2: Exterior Water Monitoring (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Enable water use tracking for heat rejection, irrigation, and water features
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Provide easily accessible, clearly labeled water meters capable of monitoring: heat rejection, external hose bibs, irrigation systems, swimming pools, water features with central monitoring system capable of hourly/daily/weekly/monthly/annual tracking

### PW-1: Improved Interior Water Use Reduction (15 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level  
**Intent:** Achieve further reductions in building potable water consumption beyond minimum baseline
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: 4% potable water reduction vs. baseline
- 2 pts: 8% reduction
- 3 pts: 12% reduction
- 4 pts: 16% reduction
- 5 pts: 20% reduction
- 6 pts: 24% reduction
- 7 pts: 28% reduction
- 8 pts: 32% reduction
- 9 pts: 36% reduction
- 10 pts: 40% reduction
- 11 pts: 44% reduction
- 12 pts: 48% reduction
- 13 pts: 52% reduction
- 14 pts: 56% reduction
- 15 pts: 60% potable water reduction vs. baseline

### PW-2.1: Exterior Water Use Reduction: Landscaping (8-10 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-path cumulative  
**Intent:** Minimize landscaping water demands through effective plant selection, irrigation strategies, and recycled water use
**Award Criteria:**
- **Plant Selection Path:**
  - 2 pts: Average landscape irrigation demand < 4 liters/m²/day
  - 4 pts: Average landscape irrigation demand < 2 liters/m²/day
- **Irrigation System Path:**
  - 1 pt: Water Efficient Irrigation System incorporated into landscape design
- **System Management Path:**
  - 1 pt: Irrigation Operation & Maintenance Plan developed
- **Recycled Water Path:**
  - 2 pts: 100% of exterior irrigation demand served via Exterior Water Allowance + recycled water mainline loop (if not immediately available, mainline must be installed for future connection)
- **Schools Only Path:**
  - 2 pts: Non-water turf substitutes installed in place of turf on playing fields
- **Cumulative Maximum:** 8-10 pts (combination of above)

### PW-2.2: Exterior Water Use Reduction: Heat Rejection (8 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level alternative paths  
**Intent:** Reduce potable water consumption for heat rejection through recycled water use or elimination of water-based cooling
**Award Criteria:**
- **Water-Based Heat Rejection Path:**
  - 2 pts: >25% of cooling tower/district cooling water make-up requirements served via Exterior Water Allowance
  - 3 pts: >50% served
  - 4 pts: >75% served
  - 5 pts: 100% served via Exterior Water Allowance
- **Alternative Path (No Water for Heat Rejection):**
  - 8 pts: Project uses no water for heat rejection (passive cooling, dry coolers, or other alternative methods)

### PW-2.3: Exterior Water Use Reduction: Water Features (4 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level alternative paths  
**Intent:** Reduce potable water consumption for water features through recycled water, water conservation, or elimination
**Award Criteria:**
- **Recycled Water for Features Path:**
  - 2 pts: Water make-up requirements for all exterior water features served via Exterior Water Allowance
- **Pool Blankets Path:**
  - 1 pt: All external swimming pools supplied with permanently installed retractable pool blankets
- **No Water Features Path:**
  - 4 pts: No exterior water features or swimming pools on site
- **Multiple Award:** Project may combine paths (e.g., 2 pts for recycled water + 1 pt for blankets = 3 pts max)

### PW-3: Water Monitoring & Leak Detection (2 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Encourage monitoring of interior water use to enable identification and correction of leaks
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 2 pts: Install sub-metering systems monitoring individual water end-uses (fixtures, equipment) with central data logging and reporting capability to identify leaks and usage patterns

### PW-4: Stormwater Management (3 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level  
**Intent:** Minimize stormwater run-off volumes and treat stormwater on-site to reduce impact on receiving waters
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Implement stormwater management controls treating run-off from 50% or more of site area on-site
- 2 pts: Implement controls treating 75% or more of site area; meet infiltration/retention targets
- 3 pts: Achieve full on-site treatment with infiltration to natural groundwater recharge or retention for beneficial reuse

## SECTION 7: SM - STEWARDING MATERIALS
**Total Section Points:** 28 | **Credits:** 18 (3 Required + 15 Optional)

### SM-R1: Hazardous Materials Elimination (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Eliminate specified hazardous materials from building construction
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Eliminate asbestos, CFCs, brominated flame retardants, formaldehyde, and other prescribed hazardous materials from building materials

### SM-R2: Basic Construction Waste Management (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Implement construction waste management practices
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Develop and implement construction waste management plan; achieve minimum waste diversion (typically 50% or project-specific target)

### SM-R3: Basic Operational Waste Management (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Implement operational waste management practices
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Develop and implement operational waste management plan with infrastructure for waste segregation and recycling

### SM-1: Non-Polluting Materials (3 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-path cumulative  
**Intent:** Eliminate use of polluting materials; promote selection of low-environmental-impact alternatives
**Award Criteria:**
- **Path 1 - Zero ODP, Low GWP Insulation (1 pt):**
  - 1 pt: All thermal insulation materials have ODP = 0 AND Global Warming Potential (GWP) < 5
- **Path 2 - Chlorine-Free Materials (1 pt):**
  - 1 pt: Replace chlorine-based materials (PVC, CPE, CPVC, CSPE, polychloroprene) with sustainable alternatives for 3+ product groups at specified coverage:
    - Insulation: 80% or more by surface area
    - Waterproofing membranes: 50% or more
    - External cladding: 90% or more
    - Floor/wall coverings: 90% or more
- **Path 3 - Mercury Elimination (1 pt):**
  - 1 pt: Eliminate mercury from all building materials (lighting, switches, controls, measuring devices); use mercury-free alternatives
- **Cumulative Maximum:** 3 pts (one point from each path)

### SM-2: Design for Materials Reduction (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Minimize overall building material quantities through efficient design
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Demonstrate optimized structural design, standard dimensions, or modular systems reducing material waste 10% or more vs. baseline design

### SM-3: Design for Flexibility & Adaptability (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Design building systems and spaces for future reconfiguration and adaptability
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Design building layout, structural systems, and MEP distribution for future reconfiguration without major renovation (open floor plates, zone flexibility, etc.)

### SM-4: Design for Disassembly (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Design for ease of material separation and recovery at end of life
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Use mechanical fasteners (not permanent adhesives) for major materials; design for ease of disassembly and material recovery for specified components

### SM-5: Modular Flooring Systems (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Reduce flooring waste through modular carpet tile systems
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Specify modular carpet tiles or raised access flooring systems for 50% or more of regularly occupied floor area

### SM-6: Design for Durability (0 pts)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Specify durable finishes and materials to extend building component lifespan
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded (prerequisite practice, no points)
- Award Level: Specify durable, maintainable finishes (paint, flooring, coverings) with expected lifespan 15+ years

### SM-7: Building Reuse (2 pts)
**Structure:** Multi-level  
**Intent:** Preserve existing structures and reduce demolition waste
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Retain 50% or more of existing building structure and envelope (by surface area or volume)
- 2 pts: Retain 75% or more of existing building structure and envelope

### SM-8: Material Reuse (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Incorporate reused and salvaged materials into new construction
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Incorporate reused/salvaged materials representing 5% or more of total project material cost

### SM-9: Regional Materials (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Support local/regional economy and reduce transportation impacts
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Source 20% or more of project materials (by cost) from within 800 km of project site

### SM-10: Recycled Materials (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Reduce extraction of virgin materials by specifying recycled content
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Specify materials with recycled content (post-consumer and/or post-industrial) comprising 20% or more of total material cost

### SM-11: Rapidly Renewable Materials (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Promote use of sustainable, rapidly renewable materials
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Specify rapidly renewable materials (agricultural crops, plant-based with <10 year growth cycle) for 5% or more of applied finishes (flooring, wall coverings, etc.)

### SM-12: Reused or Certified Timber (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Promote responsible forest management and reduce timber extraction
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Use FSC-certified or reclaimed timber for 90% or more of wood products (structural lumber, millwork, furniture)

### SM-13: Improved Construction Waste Management (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Divert construction waste from landfill through recycling/reuse
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Divert 50% or more of construction waste from landfill through recycling, reuse, or salvage

### SM-14: Improved Operational Waste Management (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Implement comprehensive occupant waste diversion program
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
- 1 pt: Provide infrastructure and operations program enabling occupants to recycle/compost 50% or more of operational waste

### SM-15: Organic Waste Management (1 pt)
**Structure:** Binary  
**Intent:** Divert organic waste (food, garden) from landfill through composting
**Award Criteria:**
- 0 pts: Not awarded
