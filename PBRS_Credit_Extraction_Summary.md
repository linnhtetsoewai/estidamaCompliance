# SECTION 1: IDP - INTEGRATED DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
**Maximum Points:** 13 | **Required Credits:** 3 | **Optional Credits:** 6

## IDP-R1: Integrated Development Strategy
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded

**Intent:** Ensure new development adopts the Integrated Development Process (IDP) as a way of attaining greater synergy between project systems, resulting in high performance buildings.

**Award Criteria:**
- Demonstrate that the design and development team and process are organized and programmed to gain full benefit from an integrated development approach
- Develop an implementation strategy to facilitate effective transition between project stages (design→construction→commissioning)
- Develop process chart detailing team structure, design milestones, workshops, and stakeholder involvement
- Narrative summarizing initial vision, objectives and targets
- Meeting minutes and action items from key workshops

**Design Rating:** 0 pts - Process chart, narrative, strategy, and meeting documentation required  
**Construction Rating:** 0 pts - Updated process chart and implementation strategy required

---

## IDP-R2: Tenant Fit-Out Design & Construction Guide
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded  
**Applicability:** Core & Shell projects only

**Intent:** Ensure that the design and construction of interior space fit-out will contribute to the overall project's sustainability objectives and targets.

**Award Criteria:**
- Develop Tenant Fit-Out Design & Construction Guide covering:
  - IDP planned for project and tenant's role
  - Sustainability vision and objectives
  - Summary of targets (scorecard with Pearl credits targeted)
  - Certification process and milestones
  - Technical guidance on how fit-out contributes to each credit
  - Owner's quality assurance procedures
- Guide must accompany lease documents to each future tenant

**Design Rating:** 0 pts - No submission required  
**Construction Rating:** 0 pts - Final Tenant Fit-Out Guide required

---

## IDP-R3: Basic Commissioning
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded

**Intent:** Ensure that the building performs as designed to protect occupant health and provide comfort and ongoing building efficiency.

**Award Criteria:**
- Engage independent commissioning agent reporting to project owner throughout design stages
- Include comprehensive commissioning requirements in project specifications and contracts
- At building completion, building commissioned by independent commissioning engineers
- Commissioning report reviewed and verified prior to building completion

**Specific Commissioning Systems Required (Minimum):**
- Heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration
- On-site energy generation (including co-generation)
- Building Management System
- Lighting and lighting controls
- Domestic hot and cold water systems
- On-site water treatment systems including swimming pools (if applicable)
- Automated control features such as blinds

**Design Rating:** 0 pts - CV of commissioning agent, contract, design reviews, commissioning plan required  
**Construction Rating:** 0 pts - Commissioning agent meeting records and final commissioning report with Owner's Project Requirements required

## IDP-1: Life Cycle Costing
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 4 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-4 points)

**Intent:** Enable effective long-term decisions about building design and construction to maximize efficiency over the whole life of the development.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating:**
- 4 pts: Conduct LCC analysis during concept design by qualified professional
- Maintain and upgrade LCC model throughout design stages in option appraisals
- Use LCC analysis to verify design, technology, and process decisions (show meeting minutes/documentation)
- LCC report required with summary history of decisions benefiting from LCC

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating:**
- 4 pts: Update LCC report with final construction costs

**Qualified Professional Requirements:**
- Chartered Surveyor with previous LCC modeling experience on ≥2 similar projects
- Familiar with ISO 15686 and BCIS/BSI standards

## IDP-2: Guest Worker Accommodation
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Promote fair labor practices in building construction.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (2 pts):**
- Develop Construction Guest Worker Accommodation Plan covering:
  - Provision of adequate accommodation facilities within suitable walking distance
  - Appropriate shade in circulation areas, parks, play fields, waiting areas (bus stops)
  - Dedicated areas for: food preparation, personal hygiene, religious services, healthcare, recreation, entertainment
  - Ongoing maintenance plan with budget
  - Inspection schedules and auditing (minimum annual inspections)
  - Formal stakeholder engagement protocol including Workers' Representative Committee
  - Workers' Social & Welfare Program addressing: health/safety/security, house rules, drugs/alcohol awareness, education, recreation, transport

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (2 pts):**
- Implement Guest Worker Accommodation Plan with evidence of:
  - Photographs showing all required facilities meet Abu Dhabi Cabinet Decision No. 13/2009
  - Maintenance visit records and inspections
  - Worker Representative Committee formation documentation
  - Workers' Social & Welfare Program implementation records

## IDP-3: Construction Environmental Management
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points, cumulative)

**Intent:** Reduce the environmental impacts associated with construction practices.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating:**
- 1 pt: CEMP prepared per EAD Technical Guidance Document by EAD-approved consultant
  - CEMP approved by EAD for projects required to produce CEMP, OR
  - Peer reviewed by EAD-approved consultant for other projects
- 1 pt: Project Lead Contractor ISO 14001 or Abu Dhabi EHSMS certified

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating:**
- 1 pt: CEMP properly applied to site with signed audit showing compliance
  - Photographic and narrative evidence of construction practices
- 1 pt: Project Lead Contractor maintains ISO 14001 or EHSMS certification

## IDP-4: Building Envelope Verification
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 1 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 1 point)

**Intent:** Ensure the building envelope meets the design intent and minimizes impacts from condensation, water ingress, air infiltration and improper drainage.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (1 pt):**
- Review of design plans and specifications during Detailed Design phase
- Off-site testing of prototypes for custom-made cladding systems
- Review of pre-testing certificates for pre-tested systems

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (1 pt):**
- On-site water infiltration testing of building envelope
- On-site air-tightness testing of building envelope

**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Ensure that the building performs as designed to protect occupant health and comfort, as well as providing for ongoing building efficiency.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (2 pts):**
- 2 pts: Demonstrate that re-commissioning plan was developed covering:
  - Final building tuning at 1 year after completion
  - Re-commissioning at 2 years after completion
  - Development of O&M Manual and Building Log Book

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (2 pts):**
- 2 pts: Re-commissioning plan updated and implemented
  - Detailed O&M manual with BMS operation and maintenance schedules
  - Building Log Book established per CIBSE TM31 or similar

**Commissioning Systems Required:**
- Heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration
- On-site energy generation
- Lighting and lighting controls
- Building Management System
- Domestic hot and cold water systems
- On-site water treatment systems including pools (if applicable)

## IDP-6: Sustainability Communication
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points, cumulative)

**Intent:** Promote efficient ongoing operation by enabling occupants to appreciate, understand and contribute to responsible resource use.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating:**
- 1 pt: Develop Occupant Handbook (printed or digital) covering:
  - Building energy and water efficiency measures and occupant behavior impacts
  - Information on indoor environmental quality monitoring/management
  - Information on building materials and environmental/social benefits
  - Waste and recycling policies, locations, sorting requirements
  - Nearby public transport, shuttle service, carpooling, bicycle facilities
  - Tenant fit-out considerations (low-energy lighting, etc.)
  - Distribution plan required

- 1 pt: Design occupant feedback mechanism providing information on building performance impact
  - Digital feedback systems/smart meters with user interface (dashboards)
  - Connected to centralized building data aggregation center or BMS
  - Accessible to each tenancy

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating:**
- 1 pt: Update Occupant Handbook (if needed)
- 1 pt: Build occupant feedback mechanism components
  - Smart meters/dashboards installed in each tenancy
  - Color-coded recycling systems visible
  - Distribution of updated handbook

# SECTION 2: NS - NATURAL SYSTEMS
**Maximum Points:** 12 | **Required Credits:** 3 | **Optional Credits:** 4

## NS-R1: Natural Systems Assessment
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded

**Intent:** Ensure that at the commencement of the design process, the environmental baseline conditions surrounding, connected to and on the site are considered and assessed.

**Award Criteria:**
- Before design process and site clearance, conduct assessment at:
  - Project site
  - Area of probable impact surrounding project site
  - Sub-regional scale
  - Regional scale

- Identify opportunities, constraints and carrying capacity associated with development
- Present findings in Natural Systems Assessment Report with:
  - Narrative
  - Maps clearly identifying extent of habitats/natural system features
  - Site photographs
  - Review against Environmental Policy Statements in Plan 2030

- Prepare measurable KPIs to guide design, including:
  - Soil, Flora, Fauna, Microclimate, Topography/views, Drainage
  - Area of site to be protected/maintained/created/restored

**Minimum Assessment Components:**
- Topography
- Geology/geomorphology
- Soils and geotechnical attributes
- Hydrology and groundwater
- Waste and contamination
- Coastal conditions
- Microclimate (prevailing winds, solar orientation, solar shading)
- Priority species, habitats, ecosystems (onsite and influenced by site)
- Flora and fauna (including wildlife corridors, seasonal uses)
- Archaeological features

**Qualified Professional:** 
- Degree in relevant environmental subject
- Minimum 5 years relevant work experience
- Proven track record on similar projects in region
- For ecological components with significant/valuable assets: habitat survey experience

**Design Rating:** No submission at construction stage

---

## NS-R2: Natural Systems Protection
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded

**Intent:** Protect significant and valuable Natural Systems assets identified in NS-R1.

**Three Strategy Options:**

### Strategy 1 - Protection (Most Preferred):
- Retain 100% of significant assets identified in NS-R1
- Retain 80% of valuable assets identified in NS-R1
- Maximum 25% of total land area consist of reclaimed land
- Protect from damage/destruction sufficient to maintain existing structure and function

**OR** Demonstrate no significant or valuable assets for protection were identified

### Strategy 2 - Onsite Mitigation (When Protection Impractical):
- Cannot apply if asset is in protected/proposed protected area, national park, coastal park, ecological hotspot, or coastal conservation zone
- Retain 70% of significant assets onsite and protected
- Retain 50% of valuable assets onsite and protected
- Maximum 25% of total land area consist of reclaimed land
- Impacts affect no more than 20% of coastal edge (for coastal projects)
- Mitigate for loss (up to 30% significant, 50% valuable assets) through onsite re-establishment at **2:1 area replacement ratio** using similar species diversity
- Provide ecological corridor between protected habitat and mitigation area
- Appoint management/maintenance organization with ≥5 year contract

### Strategy 3 - Offsite Compensation (When Protection/Mitigation Impractical):
- Requires UPC Estidama Assessor approval
- Compensate for loss (up to 30% significant, 50% valuable assets) through offsite habitat re-establishment at **2:1 area replacement ratio** using similar species diversity
- Compensation site must be:
  - Located within Emirate of Abu Dhabi
  - Degraded habitat
  - Outside Plan 2030 development areas
  - Similar or appropriate habitat type
  - Ideally surrounded/connected to similar habitat
- Appoint management/maintenance organization with ≥5 year contract (or municipality)

**Setback Requirements (Minimum, unless justified otherwise by qualified ecologist):**
- Coastal/marine priority habitats: 100m
- Mangroves/vegetated sandy beaches: 50m
- Wadis, sand sheets, low dunes: 10m
- Protected trees: Barriers at outer edge of branch spread or half tree height (whichever greater)
- Other habitats: 1m minimum

## NS-R3: Natural Systems Design & Management Strategy
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0 | **Point Structure:** Binary - No points awarded

**Intent:** Minimize demand for resources, promote soil protection and enhancement, and ensure long-term survival and management of landscaped/habitat areas.

**Award Criteria:**
- Develop Natural Systems Design and Management Strategy covering:
  - **Soil protection and enhancement strategy:**
    - Testing to determine nutrient level, salinity
    - Enhancement including water retention capacity, salinity management, nutrient management
    - Promotion of low fertilizer requirements
  
  - **Irrigation strategy:** Promotes low water demand through species selection and efficient networks
  
  - **Plant selection:** Native and adaptive drought/saline tolerant species
    - Planting groups with similar soil and microclimatic requirements
    - Low maintenance requirements
  
  - **Avoidance of pesticides/herbicides:** If required, identify impacts and minimization strategy
  
  - **Monitoring and maintenance requirements:**
    - Identify sensitive areas requiring specific monitoring
    - Establishment, maintenance, and protection strategies
    - Performance criteria for establishment
    - Maintenance duties for various landscaped areas
    - Monitoring requirements for health, species diversity, abundance, soil condition
  
  - **Management organization:** Identify monitoring and maintenance organization

- Strategy must cover all landscape areas including:
  - Protected, mitigated or compensated assets (NS-R2)
  - Areas of ecological enhancement (NS-3)
  - Habitat creation and restoration areas (NS-4)

**Design Rating:** Strategy with plans, details, monitoring requirements required  
**Construction Rating:** Updated strategy with implementation reporting and management organization confirmation required

## NS-1: Reuse of Land
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 2 points)

**Intent:** Encourage new developments to reuse land that has already been built on and infill existing urban areas rather than using undisturbed land.

**Award Criteria (Design & Construction Rating):**
- **2 pts:** Demonstrate that at least 75% of site area has been previously developed

**Previously Developed Land Definition:**
- Land occupied by permanent structure including land up to plot boundary and associated fixed surface infrastructure
- Excludes agriculture, forestry, parks, recreational grounds

**Design Rating:** Narrative of previous uses, plan drawing showing existing development, calculations required  
**Construction Rating:** Plan drawing with property boundary and final building, documentation confirming 75% remains

---

## NS-2: Remediation of Contaminated Land
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 2 points)

**Intent:** Encourage and promote the remediation of land for building development.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (2 pts):**
- Demonstrate site is contaminated through Phase I & II ASTM analysis (or similar)
- Provide Site Contamination Assessment Report identifying, estimating and evaluating hazard risks
- Demonstrate results informed overall design and layout
- Demonstrate adequate remedial steps through Phase III ASTM report (or similar):
  - Specify scope of engagement
  - Identify person/organization responsible for oversight (commencement to completion)
  - Confirm site will be appropriately remediated for proposed land uses

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (2 pts):**
- Demonstrate land was identified as contaminated through assessment
- Demonstrate adequate remedial steps taken prior to development:
  - Validation report of remediation from qualified professional
  - Confirm site correctly and appropriately remediated for proposed use

**Assessment Protocol:**
- Phase I: Identify potential for contamination through former land uses/preliminary analysis
- Phase II: Site surveys to determine presence and extent of contamination
- Phase III: Demonstrate remedial steps to decontaminate or safely encapsulate
- Asbestos in ground qualifies as contamination

**Qualified Professional:** 
- Degree in contaminated land remediation
- Minimum 5 years work experience
- Regular practice in field

## NS-3: Ecological Enhancement
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Enhance the ecological value of the site.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating:**
- **1 pt:** 50% of plants specified to comprise native and adaptive drought/saline tolerant species
  - Minimum 5 different types of species
  
- **2 pts:** 70% of plants specified to comprise native and adaptive drought/saline tolerant species
  - Minimum 10 different types of species

**Plant Specifications:**
- Native or adaptive species with proven track record surviving in similar environments
- No invasive species
- Drought and/or saline tolerant
- Must integrate management requirements (NS-R3)

**Design Rating:** Ecological/landscape report with plant palette, drawings showing location/area coverage, confirmation of NS-R3 integration required  
**Construction Rating:** Photographs and as-built landscape drawings showing planted species

---

## NS-4: Habitat Creation & Restoration
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 6 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-6 points, cumulative)

**Intent:** Restore or re-create a habitat that is self sustaining.

### Option 1: Habitat Creation/Restoration (4 pts maximum)
**Award Criteria:**
- **4 pts:** Demonstrate habitat creation/restoration equivalent to:
  - **Onsite:** Minimum habitat size for effective function OR 2× minimum landscape area (whichever greater)
  - **Offsite:** 4× minimum landscape area
  - Location within areas identified in NS-R1

- Habitat type must be Priority Habitat appropriate to site location

### Option 2: Ecological Corridors (2 pts additional)
**Award Criteria:**
- **2 pts:** Through collaboration with neighboring landowners, create ecological corridors linking habitat creation/restoration sites to similar habitat types near/beyond site:
  - Minimum 40m width
  - Landscaped with native and adaptive species
  - Create connections between habitats
  - Minimum 10-year monitoring/management strategy

**Minimum Habitat Size Calculation:**
- On-site or off-site habitat creation equivalent to minimum habitat size for effective function or two times minimum landscape area, whichever is greater
- Off-site requires 4× minimum landscape area
- Off-site must be degraded habitat, within Emirate, outside Plan 2030 areas, surrounded/connected to similar habitat

**Priority Habitat Types Eligible:**
- Intertidal Mudflats
- Mangroves
- Vegetated Sandy Beaches
- Seagrass/Coral
- Wadis, Sand Sheets, Low Dunes
- Interdunal Plains, High Dunes
- Alluvial Plains, Jebels
- Other significant habitat types

**Design Rating:** Habitat Creation/Restoration Strategy with calculations, KPIs, plant palette, habitat type justification, implementation methodology, 10-year monitoring strategy required  
**Construction Rating:** Plans, narrative, photographic evidence of implementation, documentation of failures/rectification

# SECTION 3: LBo - LIVABLE BUILDINGS OUTDOORS
**Maximum Points:** 14 | **Required Credits:** 3 | **Optional Credits:** 10

## LBo-R1: Plan 2030
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Ensure all new development supports the vision for the Emirate of Abu Dhabi as defined by applicable Plan 2030, Development Code, or Area Plan.

**Award Criteria:**
- Development located within Urban Growth Boundary identified by UPC's adopted Framework Plans
- Project's Floor Area Ratio (FAR), dwelling units/hectare (du/ha), building height, mass, transportation and open space networks consistent with applicable Plan 2030, Development Code, or Area Plan

**Design Rating:** Letter from UPC confirming location within Urban Growth Boundary and no significant variance required  
**Construction Rating:** No required submission

---

## LBo-R2: Urban Systems Assessment
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Ensure thorough understanding of project site within urban context before design process begins to inform project programming, connectivity and built form.

**Assessment Scales Required:**
- Local scale
- Sub-regional scale
- Regional scale

**Minimum Assessment Elements:**
- Distance and access to community facilities (per UPC Community Facility Requirements)
- Significant areas of public interest (beaches, cultural/heritage landmarks)
- Solar orientation and shading patterns
- Adjoining street patterns
- Access to public transit stops (existing and proposed)
- Bicycle and pedestrian paths
- Utility plots and corridors
- Job availability
- Land uses (retail centers, food provision, markets, shops, cafés, restaurants, food production)

**Design Rating:** Urban Systems Assessment Report with narrative, maps, site photographs identifying existing conditions and opportunities/constraints  
**Construction Rating:** No required submission

---

## LBo-R3: Outdoor Thermal Comfort Strategy
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Increase outdoor thermal comfort during transition months and reduce thermal discomfort during summer months in public spaces and walkways.

**Minimum Shading Requirements (% shading):**
- Exterior Surface Parking (>10 spaces): **40%**
- Public Open Spaces (privately owned included): **60%**
- Primary Pedestrian Walkways (1.8m width): **75%**
- Secondary Pedestrian Walkways (1.8m width): **75%**
- Cycle Tracks: **50%**
- Playgrounds: **90%**

**Additional Requirements:**
- High-albedo surfaces (cover/structural shading): Minimum SRI of 29
- Shade from trees measured at 3 years growth
- Public realm shade excludes active recreational areas (playing fields, ball courts)
- Calculations at equinox (March 21) and summer solstice (June 21) at 1:00pm clock time
- Shaded area must be in shade at both equinox and solstice
- Dappled shade >60% may be counted as fully shaded
- Adjacent building shade may be included

**Design Rating:** Outdoor Thermal Comfort Strategy report, drawings showing shading, plans with 1:00pm shading calculations, SRI specifications required  
**Construction Rating:** Updated strategy with as-built drawings and photographs showing shade areas and SRI verification

## LBo-6: Public Transport
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 3 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-3 points)

**Intent:** Encourage use of public transport by building occupants and visitors.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating:**
- **2 pts:** Building within 350m safe walking distance to bus/tram stop with maximum 15-minute midday single-direction frequency

- **3 pts:** 2 points PLUS building also within 350m safe walking distance to metro or regional rail station

## LBo-7: Bicycle Facilities
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Minimize GHG emissions and encourage bicycle use (particularly cooler winter months).

### Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating:

#### 1 Credit Point - Bicycle Parking:
**General Specifications for All Parking:**
- Solid stands (securely fixed to ground) allowing wheel and frame to lock safely
- Each space minimum 2m × 0.75m
- 1.5m wide aisle alongside for maneuvering
- All parking shaded
- Clear signage if not visible from street/building

**Building Type-Specific Parking:**

**Offices & Retail:**
- Long-term commuter parking: 1 space per 500m² GFA (off-street in secure room/garage)
- Short-term visitor parking: 1 space per 500m² GFA (within 30m of entrance, accessible from road/cycle path)

**Multi-Residential:**
- Long-term occupant parking: 1 space per unit
- Short-term visitor parking: 1 space per 4 units

**Schools:**
- Long-term commuter parking: 1 space per classroom
- Short-term visitor parking: 2 spaces per classroom (K-6 exempt from visitor requirement)

#### Additional 1 Credit Point - Showers & Cycle Lanes:

**Option A - Showers & Changing Facilities (Non-Residential):**
- Shower facilities: Minimum 1 per gender per 5,000m² GFA (maximum 10 per gender)
- Dedicated clothes changing areas (separate men/women)
- Clothes lockers: 1 per bicycle parking space

**Option B - Cycle Lanes (Multi-Residential Only):**
- Cycle lanes providing direct access to cycle storage
- Connecting to cycle lanes outside development OR to adjacent roads
- Minimum dimensions:
  - Shared pedestrian/cycle: 3m total width
  - Segregated cycle lane: 2m width
- Compliance with Urban Street Design Manual

**Intent:** Encourage car sharing and more fuel-efficient personal transport.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating:**
- **1 pt:** 6% of parking spaces reserved for priority vehicles:
  - Electric vehicles

## LBo-9: Travel Plan
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 1 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 1 point)

**Intent:** Reduce single occupancy vehicle use by managing demand for travel and maximizing alternatives to car travel.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (1 pt):**
- Develop Travel Plan including:
  - Details of measures proposed to manage demand for car travel
  - Targets for reducing car travel
  - Means of monitoring effectiveness
  - Remedial measures if targets not delivered

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (1 pt):**
- Update and implement Travel Plan

**Expected Performance:** Successful plans should secure 5-10% reduction in single-occupancy car use in first 3 years

## LBo-10: Light Pollution Reduction
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 1 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 1 point)

**Intent:** Encourage reduction of night-time light pollution and associated impacts on human and ecological health.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating (1 pt):**

**Interior Lighting Requirements:**
- Maximum candela angle from each interior luminaire must not exit windows
  - OR
- Manual override with automatic shutoff outside normal occupancy hours

**Exterior Lighting Requirements:**
- All non-safety/security external lighting (display, advertising, specialty): Automatically switched off 23:00-07:00hrs
- Automatically switched off outside these hours if daylight sufficient
- Safety lighting (if 23:00-07:00hrs): Reduced levels per IESNA RP-33-99 and IES RP-8
- Security lighting: Motion sensors (activate only when occupancy detected)

**Boundary Lighting Limits (Per IECC 2009):**
| Lighting Zone | Max Boundary Illuminance | Max Distance Beyond Boundary | Max % Uplight |
|---|---|---|---|
| LZ1: Parks/rural areas | <1 Lux | N/A | 0% |
| LZ2: Residential areas | 1.1 Lux | <1 Lux @ 3m | 2% |
| LZ3: All other areas | 2.2 Lux | <1 Lux @ 4.5m | 5% |

- If no external lighting: Credit awarded by default
- High structure aircraft hazard lights excluded from calculations

## LBi-R1: Healthy Ventilation Delivery
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Protect quality of air drawn into buildings and ensure minimum delivery of outdoor fresh air.

**Award Criteria:**

- All exhausts located outside defined public realm or per local code
- All occupied areas comply with minimum thresholds per ASHRAE 62.1:2007 using ventilation rate procedure or local code (more stringent)

**Construction Rating:** As-built mechanical drawings, commissioning results demonstrating compliance with fresh air rates

## LBi-R2: Smoking Control
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Eliminate or minimize exposure of building occupants to tobacco smoke.

**General Requirements (All Building Types):**
- Smoking prohibited throughout building(s) including car parks
- 25m smoke-free zones around all entrances, outdoor air intakes, operable windows
- All security staff trained for smoking control within/outside buildings
- External smoking areas away from public/high pedestrian thoroughfares
- Suitable facilities for collecting ash/cigarette ends
- Signage in external smoking areas listing negative health impacts and assistance resources

**Residential-Specific Additional Requirements:**
- All residential units: Sealed with impermeable deck-to-deck partitions along perimeter
- All penetrations in walls/floors/ceilings sealed
- All internal doors weather-stripped
- No smoking in common areas or car parks

**Schools-Specific Requirements:**
- Smoking prohibited throughout school buildings and grounds

**Design Rating:** Written owner/developer commitment (in contracts/leases), signage specifications, drawings showing smoking area locations/distances, residential unit sealing details  
**Construction Rating:** Photographic evidence of signage and smoking facilities, training materials for security staff, residential unit weather-stripping/sealing evidence

## LBi-R3: Legionella Prevention
**Type:** Required (R) | **Points:** 0

**Intent:** Manage risk of Legionella in water-based building systems.

**Award Criteria:**
- Develop and implement Legionella Management Plan for all relevant water-based systems
- Follow requirements/guidance in 'Legionnaire's Disease - Control of Legionella Bacteria in Water Systems' Approved Code of Practice L8 (3rd Edition 2000)

**Relevant Water-Based Systems Include:**
- Water systems with cooling towers
- Water systems with evaporative condensers
- Hot and cold water systems
- Plant containing water >20°C likely to release spray/aerosol during operation/maintenance
- Humidifiers
- Air washers
- Spa baths and pools
- Car/bus washes
- Wet scrubbers
- Fountains and water features (indoor/outdoor)
- Sprinkler and hose reel systems

**Legionella Management Plan Must Include:**
- Risk Identification and Risk Assessment
- Risk Management Plan (management responsibilities, training & competence)
- Exposure Risk Prevention/Control Plan
- Record Keeping Plan
- Responsibility Plan (manufacturers, importers, suppliers, installers)

**Design Rating:** Narrative and drawings of HVAC systems (regardless if cooling towers present), narrative and drawings of other water-based systems, Legionella Management Plan  
**Construction Rating:** O&M Manual, as-built drawings, narrative explaining Legionella Management integration into OMM, audit checklists (installation, commissioning, testing, training, record keeping)

## LBi-3: Construction Indoor Air Quality Management
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Implement construction practices promoting high degree of IAQ for workers and occupants.

**Award Criteria - Design Rating (2 pts):**
- Develop comprehensive Construction IAQ Management Plan covering:
  - **Source Control**
  - **Building Flush Out**
  - **Resuming Normal Building Operation**
- Complete IAQ Planning Checklists per SMACNA Guidelines
- Assessment of pre-occupancy flush-out duration based on:
  - Likely ambient conditions per project schedule
  - Most onerous ambient conditions (showing possible maximum delay impact)

**Award Criteria - Construction Rating (2 pts):**
- Implement Construction IAQ Management Plan with evidence:

**Step 1 - Source Control:**
- Design and implement measures per SMACNA IAQ Guidelines
- Protect absorptive materials from moisture damage
- Install MERV 8 or G4 filters in permanent air handling during construction (replace before occupancy)

**Step 2 - Building Flush Out (Minimum Requirements):**
- Thoroughly clean building prior to flush-out
- Complete HVAC testing and balancing
- Assess ambient humidity/temperature for flush-out period
- Maximum fresh air delivery maintaining design basis humidity/temperature
- **Pre-occupancy flush-out:** Minimum 9 consecutive days at 2ach (air changes/hour) OR until 1,185m³/m² internal floor area delivered
- **Post-occupancy flush-out:** Minimum 24 consecutive days at 2ach OR until additional 3,160m³/m² internal floor area delivered
  - Fresh air rate must equal or exceed minimum from LBi-R1 or LBi-1 (whichever greater)
  - Commence ≥2 hours prior to occupancy, continue during occupied times

**Step 3 - Normal Building Operation:**
- Reset air conditioning systems to building design basis parameters

**Construction Rating Documentation:**
- Time-stamped photos of protective measures weekly minimum
- HVAC testing and balancing results
- Ambient humidity levels during flush-out
- Fresh air delivery rate and flush-out duration evidence
- Air conditioning system reset confirmation

## LBi-4: Car Park Air Quality Management
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 1 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 1 point)

**Intent:** Facilitate adequate air quality within enclosed car parks.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating (1 pt):**

**If NO enclosed car parks (75% enclosure): Credit awarded automatically**

**Ventilation Design Requirements (Continuous Measurement):**
- Carbon Monoxide (CO): 15-minute average ≤100 mg/m³
- Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂): 1-hour average ≤200 μg/m³
- Particulate Matter PM₁₀: 24-hour average ≤50 μg/m³

**System Requirements:**
- Install air quality alert system connected to BMS
- Set trigger values accounting for response times
- Monitoring locations: Worst-case within car park (not immediately adjacent to vents)
- Minimum monitoring points: No part >25m from sampling point
- One monitoring point per ventilation system area
- Minimum one per level
- One at each entrance/exit
- Breathing zone: 1-1.8m above floor, >0.1m from walls/equipment
- Outdoor intakes away from pollution sources (busy roads, boiler exhausts)

**Design Rating:** Narrative describing strategy, drawings showing sensor locations, specifications confirming system type, extracts from specifications  
**Construction Rating:** Updated narrative, as-built drawings, commissioning report with calibration/set-points, system performance verification

## LBi-5.1: Thermal Comfort & Controls - Thermal Zoning
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 1 | **Point Structure:** Binary (0 or 1 point)

**Intent:** Promote logical thermal zoning strategies for comfort, flexibility and energy efficiency.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating (1 pt):**

**Thermal Zoning Requirements:**
- All spaces modeled to determine zonal cooling demand
- Separately controllable zones with minimum thermostat control of air speed or temperature

**Maximum Zone Sizes:**
- Perimeter spaces: Maximum 35m² open plan
- Internal spaces: Maximum 70m² open plan

**Specific Space Types:**
- Private enclosed spaces: One thermostatic controller
- Multi-occupant spaces (meeting rooms, classrooms, etc.): At least one controller
- Movable internal partitions: Controller for each space

**Building Type-Specific Requirements:**

**Retail:**
- Minimum separate zones for each tenancy
- Larger open retail >100m²: Follow general guidance
- Attention to areas with varying internal loads

**Residential:**
- Separate controls for each living area and each bedroom (including maids' rooms)
- Common areas: Follow general requirements

**Schools:**
- Separate zones and controls for each classroom and administrative office

**Design Rating:**
- Narrative describing thermal zoning strategy (must match RE-R1)
- Mechanical system drawings with zone boundaries/references and thermostat locations
- Spreadsheet listing each zone with: reference name, floor area, air temperature, resultant/operative temperature, peak cooling loads

**Construction Rating:**
- Updated narrative
- As-built mechanical drawings
- Spreadsheet with zone parameters
- Commissioning report verifying zones operate as intended
- Typical floor plans acceptable where equivalency demonstrated

## LBi-5.2: Thermal Comfort & Controls - Occupant Control
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 (Not applicable to Retail) | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Promote projects providing individual comfort controls for wellbeing, productivity and thermal comfort.

**PREREQUISITE: LBi-5.1 must be achieved**

### Mechanical Ventilation (Up to 2 pts):

**Award Criteria - 1 pt:**
- Ventilation/temperature control allows occupant control over temperature and/or air speed
- Minimum 1 control per 2 occupants in occupied area (for offices/administrative)
- Controls via:
  - Thermostatic controllers linked to on-floor plant
  - Supply air terminals directly controllable by occupants (floor level, workstation, overhead)

**Award Criteria - Additional 1 pt:**
- Occupancy sensors linked to HVAC systems
- Automatically modulate temperature/air flowrate based on occupancy
- Setback to minimum design setback in multi-occupant spaces

### Mixed-Mode Ventilation (Up to 2 pts):

**Award Criteria - 1 pt:**
- Provide operable windows per ASHRAE 62.1:2007 paragraph 5.1 for 50% of occupied area

**Award Criteria - 2 pts:**
- Provide operable windows per ASHRAE 62.1:2007 paragraph 5.1 for 75% of occupied area

**Residential-Specific (Mixed-Mode Only):**
- Mechanical system meeting LBi-5.1 requirements PLUS
- Operable windows for:
  - **1 pt:** 50% of living areas and bedrooms
  - **2 pts:** 75% of living areas and bedrooms

**Design Rating:**
- Narrative describing occupant control strategy
- Mechanical system drawings showing supply terminals and control device locations
- Plans showing thermostat locations and movable wall locations
- BMS point schedule

**Construction Rating:**
- Updated narrative with photographic evidence
- As-built mechanical drawings
- As-built plans showing control locations

## LBi-5.3: Thermal Comfort & Controls - Thermal Comfort Modeling
**Type:** Optional | **Max Points:** 2 | **Point Structure:** Multi-level (0-2 points)

**Intent:** Promote projects designed to deliver optimal thermal comfort.

**Award Criteria - Design & Construction Rating:**

**Mechanical Ventilation (All Building Types):**

**1 pt - Category C Performance:**
- Predicted Mean Vote (PMV) between -0.7 and +0.7 (15% PPD)
- Per ISO 7730:2005
- Achieved for 98% of year during standard occupancy hours

**1 pt - Category B Performance:**
- PMV between -0.5 and +0.5 (10% PPD)
- Per ISO 7730:2005
- Achieved for 98% of year during standard occupancy hours

**Mixed-Mode (Additional Requirements):**
- Comply with mechanical requirements above for fully mechanically ventilated periods
- Natural ventilation period: Comply with ASHRAE 55-2004 section 5.3
- Achieve 90% acceptability limits for naturally ventilated periods

**Design Rating:**
- Narrative describing thermal comfort strategy
- Mechanical system drawings with zone boundaries
- Simulation model screenshots showing thermal zones
- Spreadsheet with zone parameters augmented with PMV/PPD results
- (Must match LBi-5.1 zoning information)

**Construction Rating:**
- Updated narrative
- As-built mechanical system drawings
- Simulation model screenshots
- Spreadsheet with PMV/PPD results matching designed zones

**Modeling Requirements:**
- DSM software capable of hourly simulations for 8760 hours
- Software capable of calculating PMV/PPD (or use EN ISO 7730 equations)
- For fully mixed systems (FCU/VAV): Relative humidity 50% ±10%, air speed <0.1 m/s
- Appropriate metabolic rates and clothing indices for space usage
- For office work: Include thermal insulation of standard office chairs (Table C.3) unless other proven

## APPENDIX: KEY DEFINITIONS

**Baseline Building:** Building meeting minimum acceptable standards per ASHRAE 90.1-2007

**Previously Developed Land:** Land occupied by permanent structure including land to plot boundary and associated fixed surface infrastructure (excludes agriculture, forestry, parks, recreational grounds)

**Exterior Water Allowance:** Notional value representing amount of water allowed for exterior uses based on building wastewater balance

**Binary Credit:** Credit awarded as 0 points or full points (no partial credit)

**Multi-Level Credit:** Credit awarded in incremental points based on achievement levels

**Direct Line of Sight:** Unobstructed vision from user position

**Safe Pedestrian Route:** Accessible path following actual street routes (not straight-line distance)
